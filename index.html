    How to write and plan tasks well

    Capture everything, fast

    More than 2 minutes? Into Daybook right away. Less than 2 minutes? Do it now. Your head is for thinking, not storing.

    Write the next physical action

    Not "Report" but "Draft section 2 of the Q4 report". Verb + object + result, and fill in Done when… so the finish line is obvious.

    Prioritize with the Eisenhower matrix

    Do nowToday, yourself
    ScheduleBlock time — the most valuable zone
    DelegateHand off, set a check-in
    Later / maybePark it or drop it

    Time-block, don't just list

    A task with a date and a start time gets done far more often. Add ~25% buffer to estimates. Deep-focus work goes into your best-energy hours; batch admin into one block.

    Daily rhythm (10 min)

    Evening: pick tomorrow's 1–3 most important tasks. Morning: check the calendar and protect focus blocks. Leave ~30% of the day unplanned.

    Weekly review (Friday, 30 min)

    Tick off what's done, reschedule what slipped, clear the "Later" pile, and block next week's "Schedule" tasks first.

    Processes

    Your repeating work as ready-made steps. Set a process up once and Daybook puts every step in your calendar, with hand-offs to other teams tracked as "Waiting on".
